\title{Implementation and Evaluation of Dijkstra's Algorithm with Different Priority Queues}
\author{}
\date{\ReportDate}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
This project compares seven priority-queue strategies for Dijkstra's algorithm
within a shared CSR graph representation and queue interface. The comparison
includes a Fibonacci heap and also considers FIFO SPFA and SLF+LLL SPFA, although
neither SPFA variant is a heap-based form of Dijkstra's algorithm. The experiments
cover 171 random-graph configurations, 126 fixed-density configurations, and
1,000 queries on each of the NY and BAY DIMACS road networks for nine
implementations. In the WSL2 Release measurements, the radix heap was the fastest
Dijkstra queue on both road networks, with mean wall-clock times of 12.621 and
13.873 ms/query for NY and BAY, respectively. SLF+LLL SPFA required 7.267 and
9.258 ms/query. The Fibonacci heap showed no practical speed advantage on either
network. The report distinguishes asymptotic guarantees, finite-size model fits,
and measured runtimes, and it states the applicable range of the observed results.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Dijkstra's algorithm; Fibonacci heap; radix heap; DIMACS; complexity analysis
